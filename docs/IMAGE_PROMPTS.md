## medical-atlas.webp

Use case: scientific-educational. Create one refined academic editorial illustration for a Japanese researcher's personal website, landscape 3:2. Subject: reading the human body through medical image analysis. An exquisite anatomical study of the pancreas and branching pancreatic duct, drawn as fine dark forest-green graphite / copperplate lines over warm ivory paper (#f5f3eb); behind it three slightly offset translucent axial cross-section plates, etched contours rather than real CT scans, with sparse precise registration crosshairs and one small burnt-sienna outlined region. Quiet contemporary scientific atlas / university press monograph art direction, mathematically disciplined composition, delicate line weight, generous blank margins, restrained color, subtle paper grain. Main anatomy centered and intelligible at small website size. This is conceptual artwork, no patient likeness, no actual imaging data or quantitative results. No text, no letters, no numerals, no logo, no watermark. Avoid neon, glowing organs, holograms, shiny plastic 3D, robots, brains, decorative AI circuits, blue tech stock imagery, medical advertisement look. It should feel printed, scholarly, tactile, understated, human-crafted. Make a finished bitmap illustration, not a webpage mockup.

## behavior-feedback.webp — initial generation

Use case: scientific-educational. Create one academic editorial illustration, landscape 3:2, for Digital Health / behavioral science and AI on a Japanese researcher's website. Art direction: a quiet university-press monograph plate, very fine copperplate / graphite forest-green linework on warm ivory paper (#f5f3eb), tiny touches of muted burnt sienna, subtle natural paper texture, generous clear margins, restrained and precise, no shiny surfaces. Composition: a finely rendered human hand in a natural relaxed palm-up gesture in the lower right, with a small abstract circular stimulus just above the fingertips; from left to right across the upper and central image, a few carefully drawn dotted trajectories and small open-circle nodes branch, respond, and reconverge toward the hand. Include subtle echoes of experimental stimulus-response notation, repeated tiny observation marks and an understated feedback loop as an editorial metaphor for evidence-informed intervention supporting human action. Clear balanced visual hierarchy, airy scientific plate rather than a dense infographic. Do not show a brain, humanoid robot, medical cross, people silhouettes, circuit board, DNA, equations, false charts, numeric data, or architecture that could be mistaken for actual study results. No text, labels, letters, numerals, logo, or watermark. No neon blue, glow, hologram, glossy 3D, clip art, corporate stock illustration, or generic AI aesthetics. This is a conceptual original illustration, not an actual experimental figure. Match a fine anatomical engraving on ivory in understated academic style; use the same dark green and sienna palette, but make the subject human action and behavioral feedback.

## behavior-feedback.webp — final refinement

Refine the immediately preceding generated academic hand-and-feedback illustration. Preserve exactly its copperplate/graphite linework, warm ivory paper, dark forest-green and muted sienna palette, hand anatomy and position, fine feedback trajectories and open circular nodes, landscape 3:2 format. Remove every botanical branch, leaf, flower and grass on the left. Replace these four botanical objects with four sparse, small, understated experimental-stimulus vignettes: a simple unmarked card, a pair of concentric-circle stimulus targets, a short sequence of identical abstract tick marks, and a small neutral open-cube outline. The dotted pathways originate from those four small experimental cues and converge toward the stimulus above the hand. This is an editorial metaphor of observation, intervention, response and feedback in behavioral science, not herbal medicine or nature-themed wellness. No text, equations, digits, pseudo-scientific data, logos, glow or digital stock-image styling. Keep generous negative space and an austere scholarly appearance.
